Questions

How do I delete everything?

In Janus, go to Settings → Reset → Erase. This deletes all your habits, history and streaks, clears Janus notifications, and returns every setting to its default. It can't be undone.

What if I get a new iPhone?

Your habits come with you when you set up your new iPhone from a backup of your old one, in iCloud or on your computer. Janus has no account and no server, so your device's backup is how your data moves.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that notifications are on in Janus (Settings → Notifications) and for Janus in iOS Settings → Notifications. A reminder comes 10 minutes before a habit's time, so the habit needs a time, and habits you avoid have no reminders. Janus plans reminders up to a week ahead each time you open it, so open it now and then. A Focus mode can also hold notifications back.

Can you see my habits?

No. Janus has no account, no servers and no analytics, so we never see your habits or your answers.
