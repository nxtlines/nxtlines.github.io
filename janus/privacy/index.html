Janus Privacy Policy

Effective: October 8, 2026

Janus is a habit tracker made by nxtlines. In this policy, “nxtlines” (also written “(nxt)lines”), “we” and “us” mean the developer of Janus, shown as its seller on the App Store. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Janus.

The short version

Janus does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never see your habits, and we never sell or share anything, because we never have anything to share.

What Janus stores on your device

Janus keeps the following in its private storage on your device, so the app can work:

  • Your habits: their names, whether you want to do or avoid them, their days, times, and start and end dates.
  • Your daily check-ins, streaks, freezes and history.
  • Your answers to the first-launch questions: whether you’ve used a habit tracker before, why you stopped, and the goals you chose.
  • Your settings, such as appearance, when your day ends, and which day your week starts.

This information never leaves your device through Janus.

Janus also reads two things from your device without storing them: the first day of the week for your region, to suggest a default, and your device model and iOS version, only to fill in a support email if you choose to write to us (see below).

What Janus does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No servers. Janus does not send your information to us or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party services that collect data.

Backups

Your Janus data is included in your device’s backups (iCloud or your computer). These backups are created and managed by Apple under your own Apple account. We have no access to them. If you restore a backup or move to a new iPhone, your habits come with you.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, all notifications are created and scheduled on your device by iOS. There is no notification server.

Notifications show the names of habits you want to do. Habits you want to avoid are never named in notifications. Depending on your iOS settings, notifications can appear on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off at any time in Janus’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

Sharing your weekly card

When you share your weekly summary, Janus creates an image on your device. It shows the week’s dates, which days you completed, and your result for the week. It does not include the names of your habits. The image goes only to the iOS share sheet, and where you send it from there is your choice.

Contacting us

When you tap “Write to us”, Janus opens a draft in your email app, addressed to our support address. The draft includes the Janus version, your iOS version and your device model, to help us understand any problem. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send, and you can edit or delete these lines first.

If you email us, we use your message only to read it and, where we can, reply. We don’t share it or use it for anything else. Our support email is hosted by Google (Gmail). We keep emails only as long as we need them to handle your message. To ask us to delete an email you sent, write to us.

Deleting your data

  • To delete everything, go to Settings → Reset → Erase. This deletes all your habits, history, streaks and first-launch answers, clears Janus notifications, and returns all settings to their defaults.
  • When you delete a single habit, it disappears from your list, but the days you already recorded, including the habit’s name, stay in your history on your device, so past days still show correctly. Erase removes them.
  • Deleting the Janus app deletes all of its data from your device. Copies in your existing device backups are managed by Apple.

Permissions

The only permission Janus asks for is notifications, and only if you choose reminders.

Information collected by Apple

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share crash reports with us. This is handled by Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Our website

Our website, where this policy is published, is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other services. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Janus does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. Since Janus keeps your data only on your device, you control it directly. If you have a question about your rights, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above. If a change is significant, Janus will ask you to agree to the new version.
